## Question the pilot answers

On one approved export of your collection data, does a read-only audit identify three things?

1. **Which episodes are usable**, with a failed task counted as usable when its data is valid.
2. **Where each lost episode went**, attributed once to a linked operational event or a rule finding, with the
   episode-hours lost.
3. **Which failures recur** across sessions, grouped as incidents.

The audit must do this well enough that your reviewers confirm the results, and it must surface something your
current process missed. Pass/fail criteria are in [PILOT_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md](PILOT_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md).

## What Amperes does in the pilot

- **Reads** your export in place. It does not modify the source (C1), and it never commands a robot.
- **Classifies** every episode with exactly one status, applied in this order of precedence:
  `infrastructure_error`, `invalid_data`, `suspicious`, `verified_success`, `valid_success_unverified`,
  `valid_failed_task`, `unknown_outcome`. The order is defined in `amperes/quality.py`. Rule settings come from your
  agreed `rules.json`.
- **Attributes yield loss.** Each non-usable episode is assigned once: to its earliest linked operational event,
  else to its most severe non-info finding, else to its status (`ops.yield_loss_attribution`). This is record
  linkage, not root cause.
- **Groups recurring events** into incidents with status history (`amperes/incidents.py`).
- **Writes one report** that tags each section by evidence class. A metric whose inputs are missing is reported as
  "not computable", never as zero. Simulator results are not included unless explicitly requested.

## What exists today (synthetic only; not evidence about your data)

- **Synthetic 3-workcell package**, 480 episodes:
  - Usable yield 89.4% (429/480, 95% CI [86.3, 91.8]) [PIL].
  - **51 lost episodes** [RECOMP]:

    | Attributed to | Episodes | Episode-hours | Where |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | `event data_recording_failure:CAM_FRAME_DROP` | 43 | 0.441 | 6 sessions, wc-B |
    | `finding C005_missing_outcome` | 8 | 0.063 | 1 session, wc-C |

  - 67 failed tasks kept as `valid_failed_task` [PIL].
  - All 6 planted patterns surfaced [TRUTH vs PIL]. The same author wrote the fixture and the rules.
- **Injected-defect corpus**, 104 episodes [DET]:
  - 40/40 clean episodes preserved, and 16/16 clean failed tasks kept valid.
  - Each of the 15 detectable defect types caught 4/4.
  - Label flips without state evidence caught 0/4 (undetectable by design).
  - Two declarations were corrected after the first evaluation run: the simulator finger-gap range
    (`amperes/sim/runner.py` comment), and truncation checks that now ignore action streams (`amperes/quality.py`
    `C004_truncated` comment).
- **Workflow time: not measured.** No human has done the study, and `workflow_tasks` in `amperes_data/amperes.db`
  is empty.
- **Human review: none.** 0 of 56 non-info findings reviewed; 397 info notes excluded [RECOMP].

## What we need from you

- **One approved, bounded export** in the canonical `amperes.episode/1` JSONL or the CSV package. The CSV package
  is `episodes.csv` and `telemetry.csv`, optionally with `streams.csv` and `events.csv`; see
  `docs/INTEGRATION_GUIDE.md`.
  - No LeRobotDataset, MCAP/rosbag2 or HDF5 adapter exists. Mapping your format is part of setup.
- **Stream declarations:** rates, valid ranges, staleness limits. Rules check only what is declared.
- **Agreed `rules.json` and `analysis.json`**, which include your usable definition and tolerances.
- **The baseline items B1–B4** in [PILOT_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md](PILOT_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md).
- **2+ reviewers** with pseudonymous IDs, plus one decision owner.

## Shape

- **Duration:** 2 weeks (proposed).
  1. Days 1–3: baseline and mapping.
  2. Days 4–6: shadow run.
  3. Days 7–10: review.
  4. Days 11–14: report and decision.
- **Workflow study:** optional in this phase. Without it, the pilot report makes no time or ROI claim.

**Price:** proposed as a no-fee evaluation in exchange for a decision meeting and written reviewer feedback. This is
negotiable. A paid follow-on is scoped in [PHASE3_PILOT_OFFER.md](PHASE3_PILOT_OFFER.md).

## Out of scope

- Live or streaming integration.
- Any change to your data or systems.
- Simulator claims about physical robots.
- Causal claims.
- Regression tests from your logs. Imported log episodes are replay class D, so not replayable.
